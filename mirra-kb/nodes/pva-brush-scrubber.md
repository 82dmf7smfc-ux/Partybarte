## Definition
A cleaning station where polyvinyl acetal brushes sweep both faces of the wafer
while chemistry and rinse water flow over it [entegris-pva-brush].

## Physics
Brush scrubbing is a contact technique that uses chemical and mechanical action
together [rg-particle-adhesion]. Contact between the brush and the particle is
essential for removing submicron particles [rg-particle-adhesion]. It is regarded
as the most effective way to remove abrasive particles left by CMP
[entegris-pva-brush].

The brush is not primarily a scraper. Inferred, no source: it works by bringing
chemistry to the surface in a thin renewed layer and by carrying released
particles away before they re-adhere. That is why brush chemistry matters as much
as brush pressure.

Cleaning chemistry concentration drives defect removal rate, with behaviour
changing above and below the critical micelle concentration
[rg-particle-adhesion].

## On the Mirra
A Mirra Mesa brush cleaner is described as cleaning by a combination of rinsing,
megasonic rinsing and brush cleaning [pat-us6886387 spec].

## Typical values
One patent describes a brush cycle with the wafer at about 1500 rpm and a foam
brush at about 400 rpm, with a deionized water jet playing on the wafer
[pat-us6886387 spec]. The assignee of that patent was not confirmed in this
session, so treat those numbers as an embodiment and not as a Mirra Mesa
specification.
