## Definition
The load between wafer and pad is carried on a small fraction of the pad summits,
not on the nominal wafer area [jmr-2020-wafer-interface]. Those summits are the
asperities.

## Physics
Real contact area is far smaller than apparent area, so the true pressure at a
contact is far higher than the recipe pressure [jmr-2020-wafer-interface]. The
number and height distribution of asperities is what conditioning controls
[sd-diamond-disc-conditioning].

Asperity height also sets where the tool sits on the Stribeck curve, because the
fluid film is compared against the pad roughness [intech-cmp-lubrication].

Inferred, no source: two pads with the same bulk hardness can give different
rates if their summit densities differ. Hardness alone does not predict rate.

## On the Mirra
Not established for this platform. Asperity state here is a consumable and
conditioning property rather than a tool property.
