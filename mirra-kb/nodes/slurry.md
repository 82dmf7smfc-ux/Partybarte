## Definition
The fluid delivered to the pad. It carries abrasive particles and the chemistry
that softens or converts the film being removed [rev-zantye-2004].

## Physics
Removal is a two part process. Chemistry alters the top of the film, and
mechanical action removes the altered layer [jmr-2020-wafer-interface]. Neither
half works alone at a useful rate, which is what the word chemical mechanical is
recording.

Slurry properties enter the Preston relation through the Preston coefficient
rather than through pressure or velocity [rev-zantye-2004].

Inferred, no source: this means a slurry change invalidates a fitted rate model
completely. Time based recipes have to be re-qualified, not scaled.

## On the Mirra
Three platens means up to three slurry supplies, one per step
[sst-new-cmp-architecture]. Applied Materials lists silicon, shallow trench
isolation, oxide, polysilicon, tungsten and copper damascene as Mirra
applications [entrepix-mirra].

## Typical values
Flow rates in the literature for 200 mm work run about 100 to 200 cc/min
[sd-groove-width-mrt]. That is a literature range and not a setting for this
tool.
