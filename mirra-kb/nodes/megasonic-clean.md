## Definition
A non-contact clean that uses high frequency acoustic pressure waves in a liquid
to lift particles off the wafer [rg-particle-adhesion].

## Physics
Acoustic energy in the megahertz range drives boundary layer streaming at the
wafer surface, which reduces the thickness of the layer a particle must be pulled
through. A CMP integrated cleaning module normally combines megasonic and brush
scrubber processes, one non-contact and one contact [rg-particle-adhesion].

Inferred, no source: the two are complementary rather than redundant. Megasonic
reaches into features that a brush cannot enter, and the brush reaches particles
that adhere too strongly for acoustic energy alone.

## On the Mirra
Applied Materials lists a single wafer immersion megasonic module as one of the
configurable Mesa modules, described as giving maximum cleaning efficiency
[amat-mirra-mesa-200mm].
