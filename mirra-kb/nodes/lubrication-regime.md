## Definition
Which of three tribological states the pad and wafer are in. Boundary, where the
pad, the wafer and the abrasive are in intimate contact. Mixed, where a thin
fluid film about the thickness of the pad roughness forms and some abrasives stay
in contact. Hydrodynamic, where the fluid film is thicker than the pad roughness
[intech-cmp-lubrication].

## Physics
The regime is read off a Stribeck curve, which plots coefficient of friction
against the Sommerfeld number [intech-cmp-lubrication].

So = μ × v / (p × δ_eff)

So = Sommerfeld number, dimensionless
μ = slurry viscosity, Pa·s
v = relative pad to wafer velocity, m/s
p = polishing pressure, kPa
δ_eff = effective gap combining pad roughness and groove depth, μm

Boundary lubrication sits at small Sommerfeld number and gives the higher
coefficient of friction and the higher removal rate [intech-cmp-lubrication].
Hydrodynamic lubrication sits at large Sommerfeld number and gives lower rate,
because a thicker fluid layer separates the surfaces [intech-cmp-lubrication].

falls when down force falls faster than speed rises. You are moving right along
