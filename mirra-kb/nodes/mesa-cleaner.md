## Definition
The cleaner integrated with the Mirra polisher to give a dry-in and dry-out
system [amat-mirra-mesa-200mm].

## On the Mirra
The cleaner can be configured with up to four separate process modules. Applied
Materials lists a single wafer immersion megasonic module, two double sided brush
scrubber stations, and a spin rinse dryer [amat-mirra-mesa-200mm]. Applied
Materials describes the result as a fully integrated four step cleaning process
that makes the system dry-in and dry-out [amat-pr-mirra-mesa].

Wafers are gripped at the edge and submerged vertically into the modules, and are
cleaned on the front and back sides [amat-mirra-mesa-200mm].

Inferred, no source: vertical immersion with edge grip means there is no chuck
face touching the wafer back in the cleaner. Back side defects found after clean
therefore point upstream at the polisher or the handoff.

## Contested
Not contested between sources. But note that the module list above is the
configurable maximum, not a statement that a given tool has all four. A tool with
one brush station is still a Mesa.
