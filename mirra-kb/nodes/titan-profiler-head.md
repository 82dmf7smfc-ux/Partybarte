## Definition
A multi-zone Applied Materials head that controls polishing pressure
independently in several zones across the wafer, so the removal profile can be
tuned [ieee-profiler-contour-heads].

## On the Mirra
The Profiler is described as a four zone head giving increased edge tuning
compared with the Titan [ieee-profiler-contour-heads]. For 150 mm it is described
as able to tune removal to within 3 mm of the wafer edge
[ieee-profiler-contour-heads]. The same work reports that Profiler and Contour
heads made daily process qualification unnecessary and removed the per lot
look-ahead step [ieee-profiler-contour-heads].

Inferred, no source: an extra zone is only useful if the metrology sample plan
has points inside the zone it controls. A four zone head measured on a nine point
radial plan is being tuned partly blind.

## Contested
The one source found ties the Profiler to 150 mm and the Contour to 200 mm
[ieee-profiler-contour-heads]. That is a statement about the study, not
necessarily a statement about what was offered. Do not read it as proof that no
200 mm Profiler exists.
