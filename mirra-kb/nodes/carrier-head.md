## Definition
The assembly that holds the wafer face down, presses it against the pad, and
usually rotates it [book-steigerwald-1997]. A modern head carries the wafer on a
flexible membrane, holds it in place with a retaining ring, and loads the ring
separately from the wafer [pat-us6540594].

A typical construction is a housing, a base assembly, a gimbal, a loading
chamber, a retaining ring assembly with its own flexible membrane, a carrier
ring, and a substrate backing assembly whose second membrane defines several
pressurizable chambers [pat-us6244942].

## On the Mirra
Applied Materials lists Titan Head wafer carriers as part of the Mirra
architecture [amat-mirra-200mm]. Four carriers ride the carousel
[sst-new-cmp-architecture].

## Physics
The head sets the pressure term in the Preston relation, and the way it
distributes that pressure radially is the main lever on within-wafer
uniformity [ieee-profiler-contour-heads].
