## Definition
Extra dielectric loss over a dense array of features, measured against a nearby
open field area [mit-boning-cu-damascene].

## Physics
Erosion is strongly pattern density dependent and feature size independent
[mit-boning-cu-damascene]. Dishing is the opposite [mit-boning-cu-damascene].
Varying feature density and the different mechanical properties of metal and
dielectric are the leading causes [mit-boning-cu-damascene].

Inferred, no source: the practical consequence is that erosion is a layout
problem you inherit and dishing is one you can polish around. Recipe work moves
dishing more than it moves erosion.

## On the Mirra
Not established. This is a pattern and consumable effect rather than a platform
effect.

## Typical values
None established here. Quote erosion with the array density and the array size
it was measured on.
