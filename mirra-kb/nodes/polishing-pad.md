## Definition
The grooved polymer sheet fixed to the platen. It carries slurry to the wafer,
transmits the applied load through its asperities, and carries debris away
[rev-zantye-2004].

## Physics
Grooves are the slurry transport path, and groove geometry changes how long
slurry stays under the wafer [sd-groove-width-mrt]. Bulk stiffness sets how far
the pad bridges across topography, which is the planarization length
[mrs-density-stepheight]. Surface asperities carry the real contact
[jmr-2020-wafer-interface].

Those three roles sit in one part, which is why pad choice is a compromise
rather than an optimisation. Inferred, no source: a harder pad that planarizes
better also concentrates edge pressure, so pad changes and edge profile problems
usually arrive together.

## On the Mirra
Each of the three platens carries its own pad, so a recipe can run different
pads per step [sst-new-cmp-architecture]. A platen using optical endpoint needs
a pad with a window in it [amat-pr-optical-endpoint].
