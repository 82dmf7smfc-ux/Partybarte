## Definition
The spread of removal across a single wafer, normally quoted as a percentage of
the mean thickness removed. It is the standard uniformity metric in CMP work
[nccavs-feeney-2012].

## Physics
Nonuniformity is a pressure and velocity map problem. Pressure varies with the
zone structure of the head and with the retaining ring load near the edge
[pat-us6540594]. Velocity varies across the wafer because the wafer sits off the
platen centre.

Inferred, no source: a radial signature points at the head. A non-radial
signature points at the pad, the conditioner sweep, or slurry delivery, because
those are the parts of the system that are not radially symmetric about the
wafer.

## On the Mirra
Multi-zone heads are the main uniformity control. A conference paper reports that
150 mm Profiler and 200 mm Contour heads gave enough process stability to drop
daily process qualification and the per lot look-ahead step
[ieee-profiler-contour-heads].

## Typical values
None taken as published here. The number depends on the sample plan, so record
the plan next to the number.
