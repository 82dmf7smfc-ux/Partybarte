## Definition
The rotating table that carries the polishing pad. Platen rotation sets the pad
speed and therefore the velocity term in the Preston relation [rev-zantye-2004].

## On the Mirra
The polisher has three platens, and wafers move through a one, two or three step
polish [sst-new-cmp-architecture]. That means the three platens usually carry
different pads and different slurries, one per step.

Inferred, no source: each platen is its own small process. Pad age, conditioner
disk age and slurry line are per platen, so a rate excursion should be traced on
one platen and not on the tool.

## Physics
Relative velocity between wafer and pad comes from platen rotation and head
rotation together, and the wafer sits away from the platen centre so the sweep
speed varies across it [thesis-lai-mit].
