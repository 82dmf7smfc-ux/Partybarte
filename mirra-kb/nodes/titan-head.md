## Definition
The Applied Materials carrier head family named on the Mirra platform
[amat-mirra-200mm].

## On the Mirra
A conference paper on head upgrades describes the Titan head as a three zone
membrane carrier, with front referencing, a flat edge to within 3 mm, and some
edge tuning [ieee-profiler-contour-heads]. The same source places the Profiler
at four zones and the Contour at six [ieee-profiler-contour-heads].

Applied Materials names Titan Head carriers on the Mirra product page but does
not give a zone count there [amat-mirra-200mm].

Inferred, no source: three zones gives centre, middle and edge control only.
Anything shaped like a narrow ring of fast or slow removal between those zones
cannot be tuned out by pressure alone on this head.

## Contested
The zone counts above were read from a search summary of a paywalled conference
paper, not from the paper itself. No Applied Materials document reached in this
session states a Titan zone count. Treat three zones as probable and not
established until the paper or a service document is read.

## Custom configurations
Nothing recorded yet. A head with a non standard membrane or ring belongs here.
