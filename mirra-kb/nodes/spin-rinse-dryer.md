## Definition
The final station of the cleaner. It rinses the wafer and spins it dry so a dry
wafer returns to the cassette [amat-mirra-mesa-200mm].

## On the Mirra
Applied Materials lists a spin rinse dryer among the Mesa process modules
[amat-mirra-mesa-200mm]. Its presence is what completes the dry-in and dry-out
claim for the Mirra Mesa [amat-pr-mirra-mesa].

Inferred, no source: the dryer is the last place a defect can be added and the
first place a watermark can appear, so drying defects are usually chemistry or
rinse quality problems rather than polish problems.
