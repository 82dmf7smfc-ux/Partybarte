## Definition
A cerium oxide based slurry used for shallow trench isolation polish. It removes
the oxide overburden quickly and slows on the silicon nitride stop layer, which
is what gives global planarization [iop-sti-cmp-review].

## Physics
Ceria does not act only as a hard abrasive. Removal is attributed to temporary
attachment of ceria to the oxide surface, driven by the change in ceria surface
charge with pH, with strong Ce-O-Si bonding taking material away in lumps
[sd-sti-selectivity].

Selectivity is engineered with additives as well as with the abrasive. Amino
acids such as l-proline and l-glutamic acid raise selectivity by suppressing the
nitride rate [sd-sti-selectivity]. More additive adsorbs on silicon nitride than
on silicon oxide, which is the mechanism [sd-sti-selectivity]. Abrasive purity
and crystal structure also matter [sd-sti-selectivity].

Inferred, no source: because selectivity comes from an adsorbed layer, it is
sensitive to dilution and to carry-over from a previous step. A rinse problem
looks like a slurry problem.
